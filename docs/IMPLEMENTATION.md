# Implementation and verification

This file owns current product coverage and recorded executable evidence, checked against the source on 30 October 2026. Exact treatment behavior is in [ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md](ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md), the wire contract in [AGENT_API.md](AGENT_API.md), remaining work in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and target experiences in [FEATURE_VISION.md](FEATURE_VISION.md).

xFactura is not yet an accounting system an ordinary Romanian SRL can use as its only system. Three things are still missing:
- independent fiscal review: every rule is cited but `extracted`, so books run in `preview` ([ROLLOVER.md](ROLLOVER.md) lists what stops on 1 January 2027);
- the rest of the agent plumbing: booking receipts (documents from PDFs and photos, the worklist, questions to people, batch approval, templates and earned autonomy exist);
- several regimes: stock, profit tax and EU goods.

The generic kernel (ADRs [0009](adr/0009-pure-core-package.md)–[0012](adr/0012-baseline-reset-and-oracles.md)) carries every workflow below:
- **Services SRL:** service invoices in lei and foreign currency with several rates; VAT normal and on collection, its settlement and D300/D394/D390; bank, cash and offsets; deferrals, advances and accruals; FX; assets; loans and leases; payroll with D112; micro tax, dividends, D100 and D205; D406; year-end with S1005 for 2025.
- **PFA/II/IF:** single-entry registers, the fiscal register, normă de venit, D212 and D204.

The agent-first delivery order is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

## What an invoice-and-bank month does today

1. A person keeps the originals: e-Factura XML, bank statement, BNR rates file, and the VAT registration evidence.
2. They record the company's VAT status (`profile.record`), configure the bank account and import the documents. Import establishes neither ANAF acceptance nor delivery or deductibility.
3. For each invoice, a person or agent proposes a recognition with the revenue or expense account. The engine computes the effect from the rule register. Every VAT rate is currently an unreviewed parameter, so the company works in `preview` mode unless it chooses otherwise.
4. Each bank movement is explained in full, against open invoices or explicit accounts.
5. A person approves each exact effect and posts it, or a posting policy covers routine treatments.
6. The month closes when every movement is explained, the ledger equals the statements and nothing is left unposted. Foreign balances raise a warning until revalued.
7. The result is supported bookkeeping with working VAT journals, not a filed return.
