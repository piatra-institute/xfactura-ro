# Provider keys precede managed AI billing

Planning decision; this records intended direction and does not claim implementation.

## Decision

Use regular Piatra AI/OpenAI/OpenRouter keys through provider adapters. Defer a wallet and new managed-payment infrastructure to an economically justified B3 decision.

## Rationale

Accounting correctness and migration are early proof. Self-hosting must not depend on a hosted commerce relay, and inference cost cannot be assumed to fund free hosting.

## Consequences

Keep the existing company-prepaid idea as a later candidate, not a current implementation dependency. [AI.md](../AI.md) owns autonomy and the economic gate.

## Revisit condition

Revisit with measured demand, usage, support cost, funding and a reviewed charging/failure-handling proposal.
