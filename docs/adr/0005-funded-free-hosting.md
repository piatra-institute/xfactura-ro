# Free hosting is a funded subsidy

Planning decision; this records intended direction and does not claim implementation.

## Decision

Preserve the agreed free-hosted accounting commitment and define a funded capacity/support envelope before broad enrollment.

## Rationale

The user chose free hosted and self-hosted accounting. That requires explicit operating funding rather than hypothetical AI margins or a silent shift to paid cloud.

## Consequences

Pause new admissions when capacity requires it; preserve existing records, exports and continuity. [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) and [OPERATIONS.md](../OPERATIONS.md) own promises and limits.

## Revisit condition

Changing hosted pricing requires a new explicit product decision and a continuity plan; budget inputs are set from measured operations.
