# Target reciprocal engine and permissive standards

Planning decision; this records intended direction and does not claim implementation.

## Decision

Target AGPL-3.0-only for the engine/application and Apache-2.0 for independently reusable specifications/interfaces, subject to a rights/dependency/boundary review.

## Rationale

Network-use reciprocity supports improvements remaining available while an independently implementable standard encourages adoption. Existing MPL grants and third-party rights cannot be rewritten by a planning document.

## Consequences

Current license files remain unchanged. Confirm the proposed SPDX policy, provenance and required permissions before migration. [OPEN_SOURCE.md](../OPEN_SOURCE.md) owns policy.

## Revisit condition

Revisit with qualified review of package coupling, contributor rights or a concrete compatibility problem; publish the rationale.
