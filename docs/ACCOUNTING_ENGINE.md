# Implemented accounting engine

This document describes executable behavior. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) owns the full-suite target; [CONFORMANCE.md](CONFORMANCE.md) owns coverage claims. No complete fiscal profile, official declaration or annual replacement claim has passed.

Start with the [product coverage matrix](IMPLEMENTATION.md#macro-position) for the overall picture, including modules that do not yet exist. The workflows below describe supported operations rather than a full-company accounting promise. [FEATURE_VISION.md](FEATURE_VISION.md) describes intended experiences built on these primitives; it does not expand their current accounting scope.

## Company configuration and general ledger

The engine remains inside `packages/xfactura-api/source/accounting`, using SQLite/libSQL and Drizzle. There is no separate accounting package or PostgreSQL service.

Creating a company configures distinct calendar financial and fiscal years using its initial `year`. Additional intervals use `years.create`; intervals of the same kind cannot overlap. The original company field remains compatible with existing workflows. A general report always names its financial year; `books.summary` requires a selection when several financial years exist. The existing review screen switches years without combining their openings or movements and retains the selected financial year in the URL across reloads.

`accounts.list` returns the sourced OMFP 1802/2014 reference catalogue and company analytical accounts. The catalogue contains 584 account/group records, with source URL/hash and an explicit reference status. It is not yet a reviewed consolidation of every amendment for 2025/2026. That source/consolidation review remains a GL-01 exit requirement. Groups and the off-balance/management books cannot receive general-ledger lines. Analytical accounts inherit their parent book/balance classification and have their own effective date.

`ledger.propose` accepts an evidence ID, financial year, date, purpose, description, source reference and explicit balanced debit/credit lines. Purposes are opening, general, purchase, sale and adjustment. Amounts are decimal strings; stored posting amounts are bigint integer bani. Opening positions may contain receivables, payables, equity, bank and other supported general-ledger balances. They belong at the beginning of the configured financial year and cannot duplicate a legacy opening.

`ledger.revise` preserves the preceding fact and invalidates the old approval. Posted proposals cannot be revised. An adjustment can name `correctsJournalId`; this is a retained accounting link, not an automatic determination of prior-year fiscal treatment. `books.approve` remains human-only. `ledger.post` executes the exact version/hash and rechecks the source, year, accounts, date, lines and closed period. An old agent grant for `books.post` cannot execute general postings.

`ledger.balance` returns opening balance before the requested interval, interval debit/credit movements and closing balance within one financial year. At the start of a financial year, the opening journal itself appears among that interval's movements. Automatic annual roll-forward and a complete statutory trial-balance layout remain later work.

## Registration, policy and rule records

`regimes.record` retains human-reviewed evidence and an effective date for normal/non-VAT status, income-tax regime, special EU VAT identification and cash-VAT status. `regimes.read` returns the latest evidenced fact on each axis for the requested date. Missing status is null; the engine does not assume non-registration or profit taxation. These records are immutable; correction/supersession of an incorrectly recorded same-date registration needs a separate workflow.

`policies.record` retains an evidenced policy document with an expected preceding version. `policies.read` returns one key's effective version. This is a source/version registry; arbitrary policy JSON does not change calculations or authorize autonomous posting.

`rules.list` publishes the draft, hashed calculation references used by the first domestic invoice treatment. `obligations.catalogue` publishes official specification references with `not_implemented` status. Neither endpoint establishes applicability, obligation closure, deadlines, approved fiscal packages or generated returns. Signed interpretation/oracle releases remain required by [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Invoice facts from XML

1. Retain original UTF-8 XML through `evidence.record` or the existing upload route.
2. Call `invoices.import` with its `evidenceId`.
3. Read the normalized result with `invoices.get` or bounded `invoices.list`.

The importer accepts bounded UBL `Invoice` types 380/386 and `CreditNote` type 381 documents. It uses expanded namespace URIs rather than trusting XML prefixes. It preserves invoice identity, parties, issue/tax-point date, currency, line amounts/categories/rates, tax breakdowns, totals, original-invoice references and the source/parser hash/version. The additive v2 format retains advance invoices, multiple references and negative final-invoice regularization lines; ordinary v1 document identity and hashes remain unchanged.

Direction is derived from the company's identity in the supplier/customer fields. A document must identify the company on exactly one side. Deduplication uses company, direction, source document family, supplier identity, invoice number and issue year. A negative type-380 source remains in the Invoice family even though its accounting meaning is a credit. Identical semantic content returns the same record; changed financial content for that identity conflicts while both uploaded originals remain retained. A credit represented by both negative 380 and positive 381 with the same identity also conflicts, in either import order or within one ZIP. Parsed records cannot be updated or deleted.

Line totals, allowances/charges, tax bases, tax totals, gross, prepayments, rounding and payable reconcile using decimal arithmetic. XML parsing rejects DTDs/entities, malformed UTF-8, ambiguous critical fields, wrong namespaces/currencies and bounded-complexity violations. It does not fetch external resources.

The additive v3 format retains document-level `AllowanceCharge` facts: allowance/charge indicator, exact amount, optional paired base/percentage, reason/code and VAT category/rate. Currency, duplicate critical fields, bounded detail count, percentage arithmetic and sums against the header allowance/charge totals are checked. Ordinary documents without these details keep their v1/v2 normalization and hashes.

Parsing is not official CIUS-RO validation, ANAF acceptance/signature verification, evidence of delivery or a deductibility decision. Other invoice types and unsupported negative-line patterns need further coverage. Other currencies, foreign counterparties and reduced categories can be retained when they fit this parser, while their automatic accounting remains unsupported. A bounded linked-credit workflow is described below.

## ZIP archives and source provenance

Retain the original ZIP with `evidence.record`, then call `invoices.importArchive` with its `evidenceId`. The existing API and MCP expose the same command. One transaction retains extracted evidence, normalized invoices, an immutable archive manifest and its searchable entry projection. Import does not prepare a posting or approve accounting. The original ZIP and every extracted file remain retrievable byte-for-byte, with SHA-256 hashes, member path, compressed size/method and CRC. `invoices.archive` reads the complete bounded manifest; `invoices.sources` pages one invoice's archive/member origins.

The reader uses pinned `yauzl@3.4.0`, central-directory metadata and lazy sequential streams, with independent CRC-32 checks. It never extracts member paths to the filesystem. Limits are 10 MiB compressed, 100 entries including directories, 10 MiB per file, 40 MiB total expanded and a maximum 200:1 expansion per file. Actual decompressed sizes must match metadata. Safe NFC paths are bounded in length/depth; absolute/traversal/ambiguous/duplicate paths, links/special files, encryption, unsupported compression and nested archives are rejected. Stored and DEFLATE members are supported. [ZIP reader behavior](https://github.com/thejoshwolfe/yauzl#readme)

UBL XML uses the same namespace-aware, DTD-free parser and company/identity/conflict rules as direct XML imports. Signature/supporting XML is parsed with the same UTF-8/complexity limits and retained separately. PDF renditions and opaque P7S/P7M signature files remain supporting evidence. Unknown file types fail the batch. A malformed or conflicting invoice rejects the entire batch without partially retained database records. Source parsing happens outside the write transaction; authorization is checked before parsing, again at commit and before cached retries. Object bytes are durably written before references commit; a crash can leave unreferenced content-addressed objects, which a retry safely reuses.

The archive output distinguishes `invoices_imported` from `no_invoice` and reports `signatureVerification: not_performed`. ANAF's published workflow packages both validated invoices and rejection responses with signatures in ZIP files; therefore, a ZIP or signature is not itself an acceptance determination. No-invoice response archives retain their evidence and zero accounting invoices. Signature trust, signed-content verification, official CIUS validation and receipt fulfillment remain separate work. [ANAF RO e-Factura guide, response downloads](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/AsistentaContribuabili_r/Ghid_RO_eFactura.pdf)

Identical normalized invoices retain their existing IDs, facts and postings even when XML formatting or archive member names differ. Every archive still retains its own exact raw source/manifest. Duplicate invoice files may share one invoice while keeping separate member paths. Repeated/concurrent imports return the same manifest; a source identity conflict never overwrites earlier evidence. The review screen shows an expandable source-archive section only for invoices with retained origins, with original ZIP and exact member downloads. Counts identify distinct archives. No import or accounting forms were added.

## First deterministic invoice treatment

`invoices.propose` currently covers domestic RON service invoices with a single standard-VAT category or the non-VAT `O` category. Recognition, service and tax-point dates must fit the supported same-month accrual treatment. The caller explicitly supplies service/tax-point dates, treatment evidence, full/no purchase deduction, no counterparty cash VAT and the service account. This limited treatment uses 704/628 or their company analytical descendants; it does not infer that all uploaded purchases are services.

Draft standard-rate references distinguish 19% through July 2025 and 21% from August 2025 for the supported tax-point interval. They are sourced from [ANAF's rate-change notice](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Ploiesti/modificari_tva.pdf); category interpretation references the [official invoice-code guide](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/Ghid%20cod%20facturi_final%20v2.9.pdf). Advances, cash VAT and transitional reduced rates need separate treatments.

| Case | Generated lines |
|---|---|
| Normal-VAT service sale | Dr 4111 gross; Cr 704 net; Cr 4427 output VAT. |
| Non-VAT service sale | Dr 4111 gross; Cr 704 gross; no output VAT. |
| Normal-VAT purchase, full deduction | Dr 628 net; Dr 4426 input VAT; Cr 401 gross. |
| Purchase with no deduction, including a non-VAT buyer | Dr 628 gross; Cr 401 gross. |

The source tax must match the declared rate and category base at two-decimal half-up precision. Missing registration evidence blocks the proposal. A normal-VAT company also needs evidenced non-cash-VAT status. Registration IDs, rule ID/hash, source hash, amounts and explicit treatment evidence form part of the approved proposal; posting recalculates and rechecks them. Later historical facts cannot silently reuse an earlier approval.

The result is a general proposal. The human reviews its original XML, treatment evidence, dates and exact lines in the existing screen. Approval and posting follow the ordinary exact-version contract. Agents can import/propose and execute an approved version under the appropriate separate scopes; they cannot approve or change company configuration.

## Commercial discounts in the original invoice

Ordinary domestic RON service invoices may include positive commercial allowances at document level. `invoices.propose` requires the explicit `documentAllowance: "commercial_price_reduction"` classification, alongside the existing service/tax-point dates, evidence and full/no deduction decision. Importing the document never establishes that classification. Service price charges use the workflow below; financial settlement discounts and reductions granted after the original invoice remain separate treatments.

The source must retain v3 allowance details, all belonging to the invoice's single supported S/O category and rate. The exact detail amounts must total the header allowance, and, when there are no charges, `lineNet - allowances = net`; VAT is checked on that reduced net amount. A 100.00 service less 20.00 commercial allowance therefore recognizes 80.00 net, 16.80 standard VAT and 96.80 payable in the supported 21% period. Purchases without deduction recognize 96.80 cost; a non-VAT sale has no output VAT. The initial invoice posts its net service amount rather than a second subsequent-discount entry in 609/709. Later linked credits use the reduced original capacity and their separate commercial-event treatment.

The approved interpretation retains `documentAllowance: {schemaVersion: 1, kind, lineNet, total, net}`. Posting recalculates the treatment from the immutable source and current historical registrations. The existing review shows service value before reduction, commercial allowance and net service value after reduction, alongside VAT and exact lines. API/MCP prepares the interpretation; a human approves the exact version. No entry form was added.

The allowance-only branch excludes mixed categories/rates, financial settlement discounts, advances, deferred-service schedules, accrual invoice matching, zero/free invoices, FX and regime transitions. Original service price charges can coexist with allowances under the next workflow. Specialized workflows continue to reject document-level price adjustments. Header-only retained legacy documents remain evidence but cannot authorize invented allowance details. When a previously retained v1/v2 invoice is reimported under the new parser, only byte-identical source evidence plus an identical old normalized projection permits returning that unchanged record. A differing source remains a conflict. There is no automatic normalization rewrite; legacy records without retained details need a separate reviewed enrichment/migration before this treatment.

Drizzle-generated custom `0050` adds only the unsupported SQLite posting trigger; there is no table, index or column change. The trigger independently checks source/proof amounts, supported ordinary-service origin and exact ledger effects, rejecting balanced attempts to post the undiscounted amount. A populated `0049` upgrade preserves posted records, existing exact approvals, cached results and v1/v2 source hashes; a pending ordinary invoice still posts after the upgrade. Export and fresh restore retain v3 facts, proof and guards. Earlier applied migrations remain unchanged.

References: [UBL document-level allowance/charge structure](https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/billing/3.0/syntax/ubl-invoice/cac-AllowanceCharge/), [amount and percentage arithmetic](https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/billing/3.0/syntax/ubl-invoice/cac-AllowanceCharge/cbc-Amount/), and [OMFP 1802/2014, points 76–77, commercial versus financial reductions](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMFP_1802_2014.pdf). These bound the implemented design; they do not replace official CIUS validation or independent current fiscal review.

## Service price charges in the original invoice

Ordinary domestic RON service invoices may include positive document-level charges forming part of the same service price, account, tax point, category and rate. `invoices.propose` requires `documentCharge: "service_price_component"`; if there is also a commercial allowance, it separately requires `documentAllowance: "commercial_price_reduction"`. The classification is an explicit reviewed assertion, not an inference from `ChargeIndicator`, a reason code or model confidence. Interest, penalties, payments on behalf of others, financial discounts, separate accounts/tax points and mixed categories need their own treatments.

The existing v3 normalized facts retain the charge rows without a new parser version or source-hash change. Amounts, reasons/codes and tax categories must be retained and reconcile independently: `lineNet - allowances + charges = net`. A 100.00 service plus 10.00 charge has 110.00 net, 23.10 VAT and 133.10 gross at the supported 21% rate. With a 20.00 commercial allowance, the net is 90.00, VAT 18.90 and gross 108.90. Recoverable purchase VAT remains separate; otherwise gross becomes service cost. Source/header-only records without retained detail cannot invent this treatment.

The approved `documentCharge` context retains version/kind, line net, allowance total, charge total, final net and each charge's source index, amount and reason/code. The existing review shows the original service amount, allowance when present, charge total, final net and a read-only charge breakdown. API/MCP prepares the treatment; a human approves the exact version. Posting recalculates from immutable source facts and dated registrations. SQLite independently checks all price budgets, one-to-one source/detail links, classifications and exact ledger effects. A balanced journal cannot omit the charge or substitute a different reason.

Settlement uses the resulting whole invoice position. Later current-period credits/refunds use the original category/rate and reduced/increased price capacity without inheriting the initial charge/allowance proof or rewriting the source. Closed earlier-month snapshots stay unchanged. Advances, deferred-service schedules, accrual matching, prepayments, rounding, FX and registration changes remain outside this bounded charge workflow.

Drizzle-generated custom `0051` replaces only the unsupported SQLite price guard installed by `0050`; there is no table/index/column change. Populated `0050` purchase/sale upgrades preserve sources, journals, hashes, cached commands and already-approved ordinary/allowance invoices. Previously retained valid v3 charge sources become usable under explicit classification after upgrade. Export/fresh restore retains the context, original bytes and guards. Earlier applied migrations remain unchanged.

References: [UBL document charges](https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/billing/3.0/syntax/ubl-invoice/cac-AllowanceCharge/), [invoice net equation, BR-CO-13](https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/billing/3.0/syntax/ubl-invoice/cac-LegalMonetaryTotal/cbc-TaxExclusiveAmount/), and [Fiscal Code Article 286(3)–(4), ancillary costs and exclusions](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_227_2015.pdf). These are design references, not independently approved fiscal conformance.

## Negative type-380 storno sources

The additive `ubl-invoice-4` normalization handles a simple negative Invoice 380 as a linked credit. Original XML bytes and negative signs are retained. Internal line/header/tax amounts become positive credit magnitudes, with `documentType: "credit_note"`, `invoiceTypeCode: "380"` and `sourceInvoice: {typeCode: "380", polarity: "negative", lines}` retaining each line's signed quantity, unit code, positive unit price and price-base quantity. Existing v1/v2/v3 sources, hashes and approvals are unchanged. Positive CreditNote 381 inputs keep their preceding representation.

Every line must have negative quantity and net, positive price/base, compatible units and an exact reconciled quantity × price ÷ base. Quantities/base permit six fractional digits; unit prices eight. Integer rational half-up rounding verifies the resulting bani without floating point or intermediate decimal rounding. Header and tax amounts must consistently be negative or zero. Exactly one original number and issue date are required and must match the posted original. Document/line/price adjustments, prepaid amounts, rounding and mixed signs are outside this source subset.

Use the existing `invoices.proposeCreditNote`/`reviseCreditNote` commands and their current-event evidence/period decisions. The server-derived `invoice.sourceCredit` binds the original signed net/tax/gross to exact human approval. It is absent on 381 proposals. Posting recomputes the interpretation and independently checks signed proof, original references and exact effects in SQLite. The review identifies “Factură storno (380)” and the negative source total beside the original journal link and reversal lines. Amounts already paid can become a refundable position; later bank allocations record the refund without changing closed books or initiating money movement.

Dedicated `advances.proposeCreditNote` and `deferrals.proposeCreditNote` also accept this encoding, preserving their unused-advance and unprovided-service controls. They cannot be replaced by an ordinary service cancellation. Simple whole-future cancellation before service begins is independently covered for purchase/sale. More elaborate schedule/credit combinations retain their own workflow limits and need interaction proof for this source encoding.

A positive invoice with a preceding-invoice reference cannot be posted as an ordinary new service. Supported final-advance regularization and separately reviewed future-service supplements retain their explicit reference handling. Positive reversal of a storno, corrected type 384, mixed-sign storno, historical errors and annual adjusting events need distinct treatments. This adapter does not establish their period or tax consequences, official CIUS validation, signature trust or ANAF acceptance.

The source convention follows [ANAF's invoice-code guide, section 2.1](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/Ghid%20cod%20facturi_final%20v2.9.pdf), with quantity/unit syntax from the [UBL invoice quantity specification](https://docs.peppol.eu/poacc/billing/3.0/syntax/ubl-invoice/cac-InvoiceLine/cbc-InvoicedQuantity/). These bound format interpretation; independent fiscal review remains separate. Generated custom `0052` contains only unsupported trigger definitions; its ordinary snapshot matches `0051`. Populated upgrades preserve earlier records and exact pending 381 approvals; export/fresh restore preserves signed sources, credit proof and guards.

## Linked credit notes and invoice positions

`invoices.proposeCreditNote` prepares an imported positive CreditNote 381 or the bounded negative Invoice 380 above against one posted native invoice. The parties, direction, currency, original invoice number and tax category/rate must match; negative 380 also requires the exact original issue date. The caller supplies retained treatment evidence, a current-period decision date, reason and either commercial discount or cancellation. Discounts use 709/609; cancellations reverse the original service account. VAT reverses the original full/no-deduction treatment. The credit retains the original operation's tax point and rule/rate, including a 19% July 2025 invoice credited after August's rate change.

This workflow covers a documented new commercial event in the recognition month. A credit date alone cannot establish period treatment. Prior-period errors, year-end adjusting events, registration transitions and cash VAT fail closed and need separate workflows. Current-period credits can cross financial years while retaining the original journal/year/hash. A linked credit does not rewrite the original invoice or journal.

Cumulative net, VAT and gross credits cannot exceed the original amounts. The exact approval includes the prior credit state; a competing credit makes it stale. `invoices.reviseCreditNote` refreshes an unposted proposal for fresh human approval. The engine enforces one native posting per imported invoice across all financial years.

`positions.get` and bounded `positions.list` derive receivables/payables at a requested date: original gross minus posted credits minus signed settlements. A positive balance is open; a negative balance is refundable. Credits against fully paid invoices create a refund obligation. They do not create an unrelated second receivable/payable. Historical positions exclude later credits and settlements; allocation also checks later recorded events to prevent a backdated duplicate.

## Purchase accruals and later invoices

`accruals.propose` prepares domestic RON services received before their invoice. It requires retained service evidence, its date, a company-unique reference, the service period, supplier identity, estimated net amount, VAT category, deduction decision and reason. The initial treatment covers one service month and postable service-expense accounts in 611–626/628, including analytical descendants. Known imported invoice bytes cannot serve as the sole service-acceptance evidence. The accounting reference is [OMFP 1802/2014, points 53(2), 310(2) and account 408](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMFP_1802_2014.pdf); current consolidated interpretation and independent fiscal approval remain release gates.

For a normal-VAT purchase with full recovery, posting debits the service expense for net, credits 408 for gross, and debits 4428 for estimated VAT awaiting the invoice. It does not deduct that VAT through 4426 yet. With no deduction, including non-VAT buyers, the gross supplier charge is the original-period expense. Source bytes/hash, explicit dates, registration IDs and the VAT-rate rule form part of the exact approved proposal. The ordinary human approval and scoped `ledger.post` contracts apply; `accruals.revise` refreshes an unposted draft.

`accruals.get` and bounded `accruals.list` expose dated original, matched and remaining balances, including deferred VAT. `accruals.matchInvoice` prepares a retained purchase invoice against one or more posted accruals. The caller supplies receipt evidence/date and exact net/tax allocations. It supports partial matches and several accruals in one invoice when supplier, service account, deduction, tax category/rate and tax point agree. The allocations must explain the complete invoice. Existing matches, including later dated matches, reduce available capacity.

Invoice matching debits 408 and credits 401 for gross. For full VAT recovery it also debits 4426 and credits 4428. There is no second service-expense line. The invoice retains the original service/tax point and rate, while its reviewed receipt/recognition period determines this bounded deduction posting. Registration transitions and cash VAT are excluded. Invoice payables then use the existing native settlement/refund workflow. A later commercial credit links to the invoice and does not consume or reopen the original accrual again.

The match approval binds each original journal/year/hash, available balance, exact allocation, resulting balance and match version. A competing match invalidates it even when the proposed amount still fits. `accruals.reviseMatch` prepares a new version for human approval. Source/period facts and original journals stay immutable.

`accruals.reconcile` compares all dated unbilled purchase positions with the selected financial year's 408 and 4428 control balances. Once native accruals exist, a difference blocks monthly close. Cross-year matching therefore requires evidenced opening continuity; creating a new financial year does not carry its GL balances automatically. Other uses of deferred VAT and manual unbilled balances need separate supported classifications/continuity rather than being absorbed into this subledger.

The existing review screen shows service dates/evidence, VAT awaiting an invoice, receipt date and available/allocated/remaining accrual amounts. It follows original journals across financial years. Invoice headings show source gross rather than total debits of a reclassification. There are no additional manual-entry forms.

This is exact matching of evidenced purchase accruals. Same-year estimate changes and release of unbilled amounts use the separate workflow below. Opening-item migration, mixed accounts/tax points, pro-rata, cash VAT and prior-year error classification need further workflows. A generic manual 408 journal is not automatically imported as a native accrual.

## Purchase-estimate adjustments

`accruals.proposeAdjustment` prepares a change to the remaining, still-unbilled purchase estimate. It requires a unique adjustment reference, separately retained evidence, evidence/new-information/posting dates, a reason and the explicit `current_year_estimate_change` basis. `reestimate` names a positive new remaining net amount; `release` names zero. The original evidence cannot stand in for the new information. The posting month must be open and contain the new-information date. This bounded workflow stays within the original financial year; prior-year errors and year-end adjusting events require distinct decisions and workflows.

The accounting reference is [OMFP 1802/2014, point 70 on prospective changes of estimates, distinct from points 65–69 on errors and 71–74 on subsequent events](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMFP_1802_2014.pdf). The recorded basis is a human-reviewed assertion; independent current fiscal interpretation/package approval remains unfinished.

An increase posts the added expense, 408 liability and any recoverable VAT still awaiting an invoice in 4428. A decrease/release posts the inverse difference. With no recovery, supplier VAT remains in expense. The original tax point, rate, service account and deduction treatment remain fixed. No adjustment deducts VAT through 4426 or changes an already posted invoice/payable. Partial matches remain intact; releasing a remainder cannot cancel their invoice balances. A fully invoiced or released position cannot be reopened through this capability.

For example, 100.00 net + 21.00 VAT recognized in January can be re-estimated to 120.00 + 25.20 in February: Dr 628 20.00, Cr 408 24.20 and Dr 4428 4.20. A later 145.20 invoice clears the adjusted accrual and recognizes input VAT without another expense. If 40.00 + 8.40 has already been invoiced, releasing the remaining 80.00 + 16.80 preserves the 48.40 supplier payable and leaves total service expense 40.00. Original facts, proposals, approvals and journals remain immutable.

Approval binds the original journal/hash, preceding adjustment journal, all known matches/adjustments, prior remaining amounts, new amounts, signed deltas, evidence and rule/registration snapshot. `accruals.reviseAdjustment` refreshes a stale draft for new human approval. Matches and adjustments invalidate each other's approvals even when the requested amount still fits. New adjustments cannot precede already recorded events; an invoice match cannot consume an increase posted after its recognition date. Database guards independently enforce the state-bound amounts, exact lines, atomic projections and immutable links.

`accruals.get/list` preserve the original `net/tax/gross/deferredTax` and add dated `adjustedNet/adjustedTax/adjustedGross/adjustedDeferredTax` and `adjustmentCount`. Remaining amounts include posted changes minus matches. A zero remainder released by an adjustment has status `released`; complete invoice matching has status `matched`. Bounded `accruals.adjustments` returns the dated adjustment history and provenance. Reconciliation includes signed adjustments in 408/4428. Working VAT treats them as classified accrual movements with no additional deduction.

The review screen shows before/after/difference amounts, the new-information date/reason and the original journal link. A later invoice also links its latest adjustment. API/MCP prepares and revises; the existing exact human approval and `ledger.post` execute. No manual adjustment form was added. Sales-estimate changes, cross-year estimate/error classification, mixed regimes and opening-item migration remain separate work.

## Unbilled sales and later invoices

`unbilledSales.propose` prepares earned domestic RON service revenue before invoicing. It requires retained service evidence and its date, a unique reference, customer identity, one service month, a positive net amount, 704 or its analytical descendant, and an explicit `completed_service` tax-point basis. Recognition and tax point equal the documented service-completion date. Known imported invoice bytes cannot be the sole service evidence. `unbilledSales.revise` preserves the preceding fact and invalidates its approval.

| Case | Service recognition | Later issued invoice |
|---|---|---|
| Normal VAT, without cash VAT | Dr 418 gross; Cr 704 net; Cr 4427 output VAT at service completion | Dr 4111 gross; Cr 418 gross |
| Non-VAT, supported O category | Dr 418 net; Cr 704 net | Dr 4111 net; Cr 418 net |

The accounting reference is [OMFP 1802/2014, accrual recognition and account 418](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMFP_1802_2014.pdf). For this bounded non-cash-VAT service treatment, invoice absence does not postpone output VAT. [ANAF's July 2025 inspection findings, case 3](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/Informatii_R/inspectie_fiscala/Informare%20privind%20cazurile%20semnificative%20constatate%20de%20IF%20%C3%AEn%20luna%20iulie%202025.pdf) describe understated output VAT left in 4428 on supplied services. The implemented interpretation and draft rate references still require independent, current fiscal review; these sources do not establish an approved fiscal package.

`unbilledSales.matchInvoice` matches one retained outgoing Invoice 380 to posted services, at its issue date. It supports exact partial allocations and grouping when customer, revenue account, category/rate and original tax point agree. The invoice must retain that tax-point date (or be issued on that same date) and explain the complete allocated net/tax amounts. Matching creates the invoice receivable without another revenue or VAT posting. The invoice then uses existing collections, credit notes and refund workflows. A later commercial credit does not consume or reopen the unbilled-service position.

The exact approval retains every original journal/year/hash, available gross, allocation, resulting balance and match state. Competing matches return `STALE_UNBILLED_SALE_MATCH`; refresh through `unbilledSales.reviseMatch` and obtain a new human approval. All known matches constrain remaining capacity, including ones after a historical read date. `unbilledSales.get` and bounded `unbilledSales.list` return dated original/invoiced/remaining amounts. `unbilledSales.reconcile` compares those positions to the selected financial year's 418 control, including analytical accounts. Missing carried balances block close once native unbilled sales exist.

The existing black review screen shows customer, service/evidence dates, applicable output VAT and exact lines. A matched invoice identifies VAT collected previously and links its available/allocated/remaining service amounts to original journals across years. API/MCP prepares the work; human approval remains separate. There are no new manual-entry forms.

Several commercial credits are supported within the original net/tax/gross capacity. Another credit or cancellation invalidates an older discount approval, including a concurrent one. A subsequent cancellation still needs the exact unprovided schedule amounts and sufficient remaining invoice credit capacity; the engine does not silently distribute a discount over future months to make a conflicting cancellation fit.

The price change adds an optional typed interpretation to existing immutable facts/proposals and uses existing invoice-credit links, exports and recovery. It introduces no table or alternate accounting ledger. Drizzle's custom migration `0030` changes only SQLite invariant triggers, preserving the cancellation guard and independently verifying discount evidence, original source, event order, deduction and exact 609/709 lines. Ordinary table/index/column migrations remain schema-generated. Populated `0021`/`0029` books retain their original hashes, approvals and cached results through upgrade.

The existing black review card shows the commercial amount, unchanged services/calendar, agreement download, original journal and exact lines. API/MCP prepares and revises; no new entry form was added. Price/scope changes, financial discounts, retrospective service corrections, registration transitions and cash VAT remain separate treatments. Prospective unchanged-price calendar amendments use the reviewed workflow above.

## Cancellation of future subscription services

`deferrals.proposeCreditNote` links a retained CreditNote 381 to the original deferred-service invoice. It requires a separate written cancellation agreement, its decision date, `cancellation`, `current_period_event` and `cancelFrom`, the first day of a scheduled month. The cancellation removes all benefits in that monthly bucket and every following bucket, including a daily plan's partial final month. It does not calculate an arbitrary mid-month cancellation. Recognition and credit issue cannot follow `cancelFrom`; the agreement, invoice and recognition belong to the same current reporting month. All known releases must be dated on or before recognition, and no selected month can already have recognized amounts. The original contract, both XML sources, previously posted journals and earned expense/revenue stay immutable.

The future-service distinction references [HG 284/2017, amendment to Fiscal Code norms point 32(3)](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/HG_284_2017.pdf): cancellation of services adjusts the base for services not provided. This is an interpretation reference requiring current independent fiscal review, not a signed 2025/2026 conformance package. The engine does not infer non-performance or an annual adjusting event from a credit-note date.

The credit must exactly equal the cancelled schedule's cost/revenue basis, including any irrecoverable purchase VAT. A purchase debits 401 and credits 471 plus any reversal of deductible 4426; a sale credits 4111 and debits 472 plus output-VAT reversal in 4427. The original category/rate and full/no-deduction decision apply; registration changes and cash VAT remain unsupported. A full cancellation may follow earlier partial credits and must equal the selected current reduced tail. Once full cancellation is posted, further financial scope credits and timing reviews are blocked. Commercial discounts have their distinct 609/709 workflow above. Retroactive cancellation, recognised-service reversal or prior-year error decisions need separate treatment.

The exact approval binds original invoice/journal/year/hash, all releases, the original credit state, agreement hash, cancelled months and before/after amounts. An intervening allocation makes the cancellation stale (`STALE_DEFERRAL_CREDIT`); use `deferrals.reviseCreditNote` and new human approval. A posted cancellation blocks releases in the cancelled months. It also invalidates earlier approvals for unaffected releases; `deferrals.reviseRelease` refreshes them. Already approved unposted releases in cancelled months become `superseded`, remain accessible through API/export and no longer appear as actionable work or block close.

`deferrals.get/list` add dated `cancelled`, a nullable cancellation reference and `cancelled` schedule/position states. `allocated` still means recognized expense/revenue; `remaining = amount - allocated - cancelled`. Each cancelled month links its cancellation journal. The existing 471/472 controls exclude cancelled amounts and due months. Native invoice positions retain the credit and may become refundable; existing documented settlements execute the accounting for a refund without moving money.

The paid purchase fixture records 121.00, recognizes January cost 33.33, then cancels February–March with credit 66.67 + 14.00 VAT. It preserves January, clears 471, leaves deductible VAT 7.00 and recognizes an 80.67 refund balance. The documented refund leaves bank 959.67 and supplier balance zero. The cross-year fixture carries a December 2025 invoice into 2026, retains the original VAT and records the credit in its current reporting period. Non-VAT and no-deduction variants retain their original cost basis.

The review screen shows the written agreement, original invoice link, cancelled months, recognized amount, deferred balance before/after and exact lines. API/MCP prepares and revises; human `books.approve` and exact `ledger.post` remain required. Generated Drizzle migration `0022` adds the cancellation projection; hand-written trigger SQL in the separately created custom migration `0023` enforces cancellation/release invariants. A populated `0021` upgrade, balanced wrong-account/month mutations, stale approvals, exports and fresh restore verify the implemented boundary.

## Service advances through 4092/419

Advance payments, advance invoices and final service invoices are separate economic events. `advances.proposePayment` explains one complete retained RON bank movement under separate contract evidence, an identified domestic partner and expected future service date. A purchase payment posts Dr 4092 gross / Cr bank; it cannot deduct input VAT without an invoice. A customer receipt posts Dr bank gross / Cr 419 net / Cr 4427 applicable VAT. Each movement is reconciled once through the existing native movement projection. The bank may use an analytical ledger account.

`advances.proposeInvoice` interprets an original UBL Invoice 380/386 in 4092/419. Purchase invoices recognize recoverable VAT separately; unrecoverable VAT remains advance cost. Optional `paymentIds` consume the complete unbilled remainder of each selected earlier bank advance. Alternatively, `paymentAllocations: [{paymentId, net}]` assigns explicit positive net amounts, allowing one bank advance to fund successive invoices or one invoice to use parts of several advances. The two modes are mutually exclusive; duplicates are rejected. The selected amounts must explain the retained invoice's complete net/tax/gross. All earlier matches and refunds must be dated on or before invoice recognition; the original receipt tax point stays intact. Matching creates ordinary invoice-settlement links. For sales, it clears the original receipt VAT against invoice VAT, producing no additional VAT movement. With no earlier payment, the advance invoice uses its issue-date tax point and existing partial/grouped bank/cash settlements. Each explicit allocation retains requested net, prior invoice/refund counts and exact before/after net, tax and gross. Partial tax uses the original rule and half-up bani; allocation of the final net takes the exact residual tax. If these amounts disagree with the invoice, preparation stops rather than inventing a VAT correction.

`advances.proposeFinalInvoice` requires a retained final service invoice containing the positive full service and negative regularization lines. Its references identify every advance invoice. Explicit `{advanceInvoiceId, lineId, net, tax}` allocations can use part of an advance or several advances; each source must be fully settled and have sufficient unused/uncredited capacity. Purchase recognition debits the full service cost, credits 4092 for consumed cost and recognizes only residual supplier payable/input VAT. Sales debit 419 for consumed net, recognize the full service revenue and retain only residual customer receivable/output VAT. A fully covered, zero-payable final invoice is supported. No source or previous posting is rewritten.

Unused invoiced amounts use `advances.proposeCreditNote`: the original invoice, retained credit, separate cancellation agreement and current-period decision determine exact 4092/419/VAT reversal. Existing positions become refundable and ordinary settlements record the refund. Used amounts cannot be credited as unused. An unbilled bank advance uses `advances.proposeRefund`, with an opposite retained movement and separate current agreement; supplier refunds reduce gross 4092, while customer refunds reverse 419 and original receipt VAT. Omit `net` to return the exact remaining advance, or supply a positive `net` for a partial refund. Corrections to final invoices remain a distinct treatment.

A partial refund calculates VAT from the explicitly documented net amount at the original retained tax-point rule, using half-up bani. It must explain the entire bank movement and cannot exceed the remaining net, VAT, gross or control balance. The final refund takes the exact remaining tax rather than losing a rounding residual. Purchases recognize no deductible VAT on a pre-invoice refund; their control is gross 4092. Sales reduce net 419 and the original receipt VAT in 4427. An original 100.00 + 21.00 receipt with a separately evidenced 30.00 + 6.30 refund leaves 70.00 + 14.70 for a later invoice. That invoice clears only the remaining bank advance without repeating sales VAT; the eventual final service uses that remaining invoiced capacity.

Further refunds require separate agreements and complete bank movements. Earlier agreements, original/previous bank evidence and the payment contract cannot substitute for the new agreement. Every partial or subsequent effect retains optional `refundBasis` with requested net, prior count/journal/year and exact before/after amounts. The full state hash binds all immutable refunds and invoice matches. Legacy full refunds omit the added context and preserve their original hashes/approvals. After partial invoicing, this refund path can return only the unbilled balance; matched amounts remain owned by the original invoice/credit/settlement workflow. Such refunds also retain the prior invoice count and latest invoice/journal/financial-year link. Matched invoice XML and treatment evidence cannot replace the separate refund agreement. The engine retains stale/inconsistent source documents rather than silently replacing them.

Partially refunded or invoiced payments remain `open`, including in `onlyOpen` pages. The filter compares cumulative matched and refunded gross at the requested date. Optional fields expose remaining net/tax, cumulative `invoicedNet/Tax/Gross`, ordered `invoices` with journal/financial-year/date/amounts, refunded amounts and each refund's journal/financial year, date and source. The legacy `invoiceId` identifies the latest matching invoice; it no longer implies that the whole bank advance was consumed. The existing `refundJournalId` refers to the latest individual refund; dated positions exclude later refunds and invoice matches. New-year processing requires explicit reduced 4092/419 opening continuity. Prior-year VAT stays unchanged while the new refund is recognized in its current period. Both refund preparation and invoice matching inspect the full intervening VAT/cash-VAT registration path, so a change away and back cannot hide behind matching endpoint values.

The review screen retains the original advance link and shows exact balances before/after each partial invoice allocation or refund, plus links to the preceding refund and latest matched invoice when present. API/MCP prepares the amount and source; exact human approval records the ledger effect and reconciles the retained movement. No entry form or payment instruction was added. A tax-rounding residual that conflicts with later invoice bytes requires explicit review/separately supported correction; matching never invents VAT to make the source fit. Automatic final-service rate/regime transitions remain unsupported; the bounded refund workflow retains its original 19%/21% rule.

Drizzle-generated `0045` changes the refund primary key to company/movement and adds a payment lookup index, copying all existing columns/foreign keys. Generated custom `0044` temporarily drops only the six triggers referencing the rebuilt table; `0046` restores them with cumulative refund and remaining-invoice guards. All migration statements run through the existing transaction-based migrator. Populated `0025` and `0043` purchase/sale books preserve posted legacy refunds, source bytes/hashes, journals, command results and already-approved full refunds. Earlier applied migrations remain unchanged.

Drizzle-generated `0048` rebuilds only the invoice-match table, extends its primary key to company/payment/invoice and adds a payment lookup index. Generated custom shells `0047`/`0049` temporarily drop and restore the seven referencing SQLite triggers, including cumulative invoice/refund budgets and exact allocation checks. Populated `0025`/`0046` purchase/sale upgrades retain all existing records and execute already-approved whole-payment invoices without changing their hashes. Legacy full-payment proposal bodies omit the new allocation context. Earlier applied migrations remain unchanged.

A 100.00 + 21.00 bank advance can fund a first 30.00 + 6.30 invoice and a second 50.00 + 10.50 invoice, leaving only 20.00 + 4.20 unbilled and refundable. After that refund, a zero-payable final service invoice references both advance invoices, recognizes 80.00 service and clears their controls. Customer VAT totals 16.80 across the receipt/refund/final chain. Purchases deduct each invoiced VAT amount only where the retained treatment permits it. No earlier receipt, invoice or closed-month snapshot changes.

`getPayment`/`payments` and `get`/`list` return dated cash/invoice positions. `reconcile` compares their remaining amounts to year-scoped 4092/419, including analytical controls and explicit opening carry. An unexplained balance blocks monthly close even if no native advance exists; arbitrary manual control-account postings do not create itemized positions. Selected approvals bind source journals/years/hashes, matched payment state and available net/tax/gross. Competing matches, uses or credits invalidate approval and require revision. SQLite guards independently protect consumption, exact effects, atomic projections, movement reuse and immutable history. Export and restore include all five advance projections.

Coverage remains domestic RON services in 628/704 or their analytical accounts, one standard S or O category, full/no purchase deduction and stable non-cash-VAT registration. The draft rate catalogue covers 19%/21% dates in 2025/2026. Mixed categories, rate/registration transitions, FX/EU treatments, separate credit-plus-final regularization, discounts and rounding differences fail closed. An expected service date documents the contract; it does not prove delivery. A bank receipt does not prove tax deductibility or authorize moving funds.

References: [OMFP 1802/2014, accounts 4092/419](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/OMFP_1802_2014.pdf), [Fiscal Code Article 282(2), earlier invoice/receipt tax points](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/L_227_2015.pdf), and [Fiscal Code norms, point 95(2), final-invoice regularization and prior-invoice references](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/HG_1_2016_norme%20CF.pdf). These are retained design references; independent current fiscal interpretation/package approval remains pending.

The existing black review screen shows contract, future service date, linked payments, available/used/remaining advances, refund/cancellation evidence and exact lines. Original journals remain reachable by keyboard across years. API/MCP prepares/revises; human exact-version approval and posting remain separate. No advance-entry forms were added.

## Bank, cash and native settlements

`treasury.accounts` reads company mappings; human-only `treasury.createAccount` maps each RON bank/cash identifier to a distinct postable 5121/5311 account or analytical descendant. The initial CSV bank can retain the compatible 5121 mapping; additional banks need explicit mappings. Legacy mappings preserve the earliest retained statement year, including years before the company's initial reporting selection. The original single-bank table remains only for upgrade compatibility. Account closure, remapping and currency accounts need separate lifecycle work.

`treasury.recordCash` retains a documented receipt/payment fact with source identity, date, amount and evidence. It does not post or initiate a payment. Repeated identical facts deduplicate; conflicting source IDs fail. Posting simulates daily cash balances, including later recorded entries, and rejects a negative balance.

`bank.importFile` adds complete retained RON MT940 and camt.053.001.02/.08 sources alongside CSV. The immutable manifest records parser/input/source hashes, bank statement identity, booked balances, source interval, booking/value dates, original references, reversal flags and nested transaction details. File parsing precedes the writer transaction; movements, statement links and immutable source projections commit atomically. Stable bank references reuse movement IDs across formats, including exact compatible CSV references. Unidentified overlapping movements stop for review; an accepted file never silently omits a failed row. See [bank API workflow and bounds](AGENT_API.md#bank-file-workflow).

MT940 uses final opening/closing balances, separate booking/value dates and exact C/D/RC/RD directions. Missing booking dates are inferred only for an unambiguous same-day statement; fragmented/intermediate statements are unsupported. camt uses booked `Ntry` entries and `OPBD`/`CLBD`, never available balances; child transactions and reported charges do not become extra movements. DateTimes retain their source calendar dates and offsets, avoiding host-timezone shifts. Whole-day `FrToDt` intervals accept an exclusive following midnight. Supported dialects exclude pending/paginated reports, foreign currency and report ranges whose offset changes. The UI adds paged, read-only source details and original downloads; import remains API/MCP-first.

Format references are [BCR's MT guide](https://e-bcr.bcr.ro/smartoffice/Ghid_formate_MT_ro.PDF), [BT's daily-MT940 guidance](https://intreb.bancatransilvania.ro/cum-pot-descarca-tranzactiile-din-aplicatia-bt-ultra-web-si-ce-formate/), [Handelsbanken's camt.053 specification](https://www.handelsbanken.com/tron/xgpu/info/contents/v1/document/72-113087) and [Dutch Payments Association's camt guide](https://www.betaalvereniging.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/IG-Bank-to-Customer-Statement-CAMT-053-v1-1.pdf). Synthetic fixtures establish the implemented subset, not bank certification or official ISO validation; consented vendor samples remain necessary before claiming a bank-specific adapter.

`settlements.propose` explains one complete retained movement with native invoice allocations and optional documented 627 bank fees. It supports partial and grouped payments/receipts, signed refunds and separate or netted bank fees, including posted advance invoices. An allocation is positive when it clears the invoice and negative for a refund; movement direction also depends on purchase/sale. Payments before invoice use the distinct advance workflow above. Mixed-direction netting, FX and ambiguous overpayments are excluded. The bank/cash ledger account comes from the retained mapping rather than a global 5121 constant.

The settlement becomes an ordinary exact-version proposal: human `books.approve`, then scoped `ledger.post`. Its snapshot binds movement evidence, mappings, open positions, fees and cash state. New payments/credits invalidate it; `settlements.revise` refreshes it. Posting inserts movement and invoice-allocation links atomically through guarded journal projections. Legacy matching and native settlement cannot reconcile the same movement twice. Neither capability authorizes moving funds.

The existing screen shows source invoices, adjustment reasons, signed VAT corrections, settlement allocations, fees and exact lines. It follows original journals across financial years and sums analytical bank accounts for the owner balance. Agents prepare through API/MCP; no new manual configuration dashboard was introduced.

## Working VAT registers

`vat.entries` provides bounded source-linked purchase/sale invoice rows; `vat.unbilledSales` separately pages the original unbilled-service recognition rows. `vat.summary` combines their movements by direction, category and rate. Credits have signed negative amounts in their recognition period and retain the original tax-point/rate. Supplier VAT and deductible input VAT remain separate for non-VAT buyers and purchases with no deduction. Rows retain source/proposal/rule hashes, registration IDs and journal references; invoice rows also retain their invoice hashes.

An invoice matched to previously recognized sales has zero additional base, tax and gross movements in `vat.entries`. Its additive `previousRecognition` object retains the invoice amounts and original unbilled-service IDs. The original base/output tax appears in `vat.unbilledSales` at service recognition. `vat.unbilledSaleAdjustments` separately retains signed estimate changes when present. `vat.summary.entryCount` counts these movements as well as the original source and informational invoice-match rows; `unbilledSaleEntryCount` identifies the service-recognition count. This prevents both cross-year and same-year double counting while retaining invoice provenance.

The summary reconciles deductible input/output VAT to non-opening period movements in 4426/4427, including their analytical accounts. Opening balances are excluded. Unposted imported invoices, unclassified journals and VAT differences produce explicit review issues. A stable hash binds the report to the checked source/posting state. `reconciled` describes this technical working register only: the output explicitly has `officialDeclaration: false` and a bounded treatment scope. It does not establish D300/D394/D406 generation, legal applicability or full VAT coverage.

`vat.advancePayments` pages pre-invoice bank advances and their full/partial refunds. Customer receipt/refund rows contribute signed base/output VAT in their actual periods; supplier cash rows contribute no deductible VAT. A later matched sales advance invoice contributes zero extra VAT and retains `previousAdvanceRecognition` references. Final invoices contribute only residual base/VAT, so advance plus final equals the full service. `advancePaymentEntryCount` identifies these rows in the summary. These working rows do not implement official D300/D394 mappings.

VAT deductions/pro-rata beyond full/no deduction, cash VAT, advance rate/registration transitions, financial discounts, mixed-category price adjustments and broader period treatments and FX/EU treatment remain step-2/3 work. Generic journals do not constitute a complete inventory, payroll or tax subsystem.

## Closing, preservation and upgrade

Month-close checks use one configured financial year and recognize general opening positions. Every active bank needs continuous statement coverage, either one statement or a gap-free daily/short-period chain; each bank reconciles to its own mapped ledger account at statement/month checkpoints. Native and legacy allocations both satisfy movement reconciliation. Cash accounts have daily nonnegative-balance checks. An unconfigured bank/cash ledger account, unmatched movement or imported invoice without its posted interpretation blocks close. A header-only statement can evidence a month without movements. Closing remains a ledger lock, not a statement that all declarations or annual obligations are fulfilled.

The company export includes accounting years, chart, registration events, policies, imported invoice facts, proposals, approvals, postings, invoice/credit/settlement/purchase-accrual/unbilled-sale/subscription/advance links, archive manifests/member origins, bank-file manifests/source entries, treasury mappings and original bytes.
