## Minimum obligation matrix for the narrow profile

Evaluate one SRL using double-entry accounting under OMFP 1802/2014, calendar financial/fiscal year, RON domestic services and ordinary quarterly profit-tax computation. Exclude normal/special VAT registration, foreign transactions, employees and other payroll-like remuneration, stock, depreciable assets, cash-register operations and distributions during the claimed interval. Opening capital, supported accruals, bank charges, corrections and evidenced tax history remain relevant.

| Requirement | Coverage needed before full-year replacement |
|---|---|
| Registers | Registrul-jurnal, Registrul-inventar, Cartea mare, trial balance and period-appropriate Registrul de evidență fiscală, with source retrieval. |
| Profit tax | Applicable D100 quarterly positions/payments and annual D101, including approved fiscal adjustments/loss vintages. |
| SAF-T | Applicable periodic D406 and an explicit determination for annual assets reporting, including supported empty/non-applicable evidence. |
| Invoicing | In-scope issued e-Factura transmission, retained exact payload and responses. |
| Annual accounting | Inventory evidence, applicable financial-statement forms, disclosures/supporting documents, management approval, required signatures and receipt tracking. |
| Conditional triggers | D700, D301/D390, D112, D205/D207, D107 or other obligations must be derived when their facts arise; they are not assumed absent from a zero row. |
| Authority and continuity | Identified preparer/reviewer, appropriate professional/organizational authority, management responsibility, authorized submitter and responsibility for pending obligations. |

Accounting micro-entity classification is distinct from microenterprise income taxation. Select the annual form/category and audit/supporting-document requirements from the actual accounting size criteria and applicable reporting cycle.

## Register and source-version contract

A fiscal-register artifact records its governing format/rule revision, covered interval, tax-position revision, chronological explanations and linked accounting accounts/evidence. Its approved totals reconcile to declarations, tax-payable positions and payments. Preserve legacy registers across the 2026 change; historical output/correction format choices need an explicit reviewed transition rule.

Each source entry needs source bytes/hash, issuer, clause, publication date, effective dates, affected fiscal years/intervals, interpretation/expected-case approvals and supersession references. A filename containing an older year or an apparently current consolidation is not proof of the applicable rule version. Resolve amendments and source conflicts before release.

The review's five worked examples are maintained as [draft fiscal fixtures](FISCAL_FIXTURES.md). Numerical arithmetic can be checked here, but only independent fiscal review can promote them into an oracle.

## Verification still required before claims

Complete the clause-to-output/test matrix; obtain exact official form/validator versions and redistribution permissions; verify annual supporting documents and signing procedures; test statutory calendars and exceptions; and retain signed reviewer approvals. A planning source check is not professional certification.

Future obligations need supported procedures and assigned actors. Acceptance of a not-yet-due future filing cannot be required before onboarding, but an unpublished future form must not be described as already generated/validated. Explicitly scoped production/shadow claims and any external annual responsibility remain visible until the full-year gate is satisfied.
