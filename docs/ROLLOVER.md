# Yearly rule rollover

The rollover checks which rules can resolve at the start of a new year. An open-ended version stops after its `reviewedThrough` date (`BEYOND_HORIZON`); a fixed interval includes its `to` date and then leaves `NO_VERSION` unless another version covers the requested date and scope. Renew a key by checking the applicable acts and recording a cited new version or a renewed review horizon through the rule-review process; only the accountant and expert raise review status. A gap remains a `RULE_GAP` in any treatment that reads it, using the key's declared date axis, and blocks work that requires that treatment. From the repository root, `bun run docs:rollover` prints and replaces the table below; `bun scripts/rules/rollover.ts --date 2027-01-01 --check docs/ROLLOVER.md` checks it without writing. For another date use `--date YYYY-MM-DD`, optionally with `--markdown`; omitting the date selects the next 1 January in Europe/Bucharest. The running engine's `rules.horizon {days}` lists open-ended horizons through its cutoff, including overdue ones; it does not list fixed intervals that end.

## What the table cannot decide

The December publication watch is about acts to read, not forecast values. Renew stable rules by confirming that no applicable amendment changes them; a minister's statement or a press report cannot renew a rule.

- **Minimum wage decision for 2027:** `ro.payroll.minimum_wage` and `ro.min_wage.contributions` need the government decision setting the wage, including the wage on 1 January used for independent-income bases. Check its consequences for `ro.sick.base_cap`, `ro.cas.independent`, `ro.cass.independent`, `ro.cass.option`, `ro.cass.other`, `ro.pit.norma`, `ro.payroll.personal_deduction`, `ro.payroll.supplementary_deduction`, `ro.payroll.benefit.limits`, `ro.payroll.voucher.limits` and `ro.garnishment.limits`: housing, holiday vouchers, the protected net wage and D112's D17 ceiling depend on it. `ro.payroll.minimum_wage_relief` separately needs the act extending or replacing the relief after its fixed interval; a wage decision alone does not do that.
- **Fiscal measures and the state and social-insurance budget laws for 2027:** check the Codul fiscal amendments against `ro.vat.rate`, `ro.vat.rate.reduced`, `ro.micro.rate`, `ro.micro.revenue_limit_eur`, `ro.dividend.tax_rate`, `ro.interest.tax_rate`, `ro.rent.tax_rate`, `ro.rent.expense_rate`, `ro.ip.tax_rate`, `ro.ip.expense_rate`, `ro.nonresident.tax_rate` and `ro.nonresident.certificate_days`; the PIT, CAS/CASS, payroll and profit-tax keys; and the fiscal determinations and filing deadlines they affect. For sick leave, also obtain the amendments to the norms of OUG nr. 158/2005, beyond the stored 2018 text. The governing clauses are in the generated rows, rather than a second list of rates and thresholds here.
- **Expiring fiscal provisions:** `ro.profit.minimum_tax` needs the 2027 reading of Codul fiscal art. 18^1, including alin. (17), and `ro.profit.superaccelerated` the act extending or replacing art. 28 alin. (8^1). The engine already reads the minimum-turnover-tax rule to check whether a company falls outside the supported profit-tax scope; expiry is not a zero tax. `ro.vat.reverse_charge.domestic` needs the act extending art. 331 alin. (2) lit. c)–f) and i)–l) beyond the limit in alin. (6), or a reviewed version recording the categories left without an extension; the resolver assumes neither outcome.
- **Voucher indexation orders:** `ro.payroll.voucher.limits` needs the joint orders under Legea nr. 165/2018 art. 32. The version after the holding period in Legea nr. 201/2025 art. II records the meal-voucher amount as a floor (`mealFloorOnly`), not a reviewed indexed maximum. Extending its horizon alone would not establish the missing maximum.
- **Other annual acts and foreign rates:** `ro.intrastat.thresholds` needs INS's order for the 2027 thresholds. `ro.halfyear.report` needs the minister's order for 30 June 2027, or a reviewed determination that none applies. `ro.deadline.S1005`, `ro.deadline.S1003`, `ro.deadline.S1046` and `ro.deadline.D406.assets` need the order for FY2026 statements checked against the law's deadline readings. `ro.vat.oss.rates` needs the Member States' rate changes, retrieved from TEDB for 1 January and each quarter's first day; `ro.d406.oss` also needs the corresponding SAF-T mapping checked.

Before renewing the affected keys, obtain or check OMFP nr. 1.802/2014 amendments after the stored consolidation and OMF nr. 2.861/2009 for stock, production and year-end; OMFP nr. 2.634/2015 annexes 2–3 for stock forms; consolidated OMFP nr. 103/2016, OUG nr. 41/2022, the high-fiscal-risk goods orders and customs procedures for cross-border goods; OUG nr. 28/1999 amendments, HG nr. 479/2003, OPANAF nr. 4.156/2017, A4203 and CIUS-RO for retail; and later amendments to OPANAF nr. 1.612/2018 and nr. 1.613/2018 for the tax account, including any government decision under Codul de procedură fiscală art. 173 alin. (6). These are acquisition/review tasks, not assertions that an amendment exists. Compare a new SAF-T definitions workbook sheet by sheet, including Livrari, Achizitii baserate, assets and WHT, and review changed ANAF answers and examples before changing codes.

A close performed in 2027 can still resolve rules on 31 December 2026; the execution date does not move a key's date axis. Likewise, profit-tax loss vintages use the loss year's rules. The provisional pro-rata for 2027 is a company fact to communicate for that year, not an automatic carry-over of the definitive 2026 calculation; [ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md](ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md) owns these workflow details.

### Return layouts to pin

Check ANAF's packages for the first 2027 periods of D300, D301, D390, D394, D112, D406, D100 and D398, and the applicable D205/D207 reporting year. A D300 order can change non-payer, capital-goods, pro-rata, margin-scheme and OSS mappings; D112 must be pinned by payroll period, and D406 needs its definitions workbook and dedicated validator as well as its schema. Record packages and hashes in [sources.json](../scripts/validators/sources.json) and bind each format to its reporting period. A period without a pinned format remains `FORMAT_GAP`; pinning a format does not renew its rules.

Keep filing year and income/financial year distinct: the FY2026 S1005/S1003 statements, S1046 inactivity declaration, D212 for 2026 income and D101 for FY2026 are filed in 2027 and need their own applicable packages. S1046's broadly dated structure does not authorize an unread year's ANAF package. Check the minister's half-year reporting order separately. Never infer an annual layout from the previous year's form; the format registry and [ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md](ACCOUNTING_ENGINE.md) own current format coverage.
